### Race to A&E: six European capitals
<p class="meta">2026 · Python, OSMnx, SciPy, GHSL, Eurostat GISCO, deck.gl</p>

How quickly can people reach an emergency department by car in London, Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Rome and Athens? Each city is defined the same way (the UN/Eurostat urban centre), drive times are measured along every road from 100 m population cells to the nearest A&E, and an animated map shows the network lighting up from every hospital at once, with busy-traffic and click-anywhere views.
